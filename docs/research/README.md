# Research reports

A report here is the finished, English, dated record of a piece of research that an issue or an
ADR cites: a measurement, a survey of sources, a comparison of designs. Like an audit report it
is a point-in-time record, so it is never edited to stay current. A later report supersedes it
and says so in its first lines.

- **Name:** `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`, dated by when the research was done.
- **Language:** English, as everything published is
  ([ADR-0079](../decisions/0079-each-kind-of-project-information-has-one-home.md)).
- **When:** only when an issue or ADR needs to cite it. A draft lives in
  `docs/local/research/<topic>/` until then, in any language, and most drafts are never
  published. Their conclusions go into the issue instead.
- **What it holds:** the question, the method (the queries or commands, so a reader can rerun
  them), the result with its date, and what it does not show. A report states no plan; the plan
  is the issue that cites it.
