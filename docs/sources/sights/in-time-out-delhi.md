# Time Out Delhi

The Delhi edition of Time Out, in English; the survey records it as written in Delhi. Looked at
on 2026-10-02 because the Delhi survey read it as its guide from inside the city. Time Out is a
London company listed on AIM; its "international network of local experts" writes the editions
(its Editorial Guidelines).

**What it sorts its places into.** "Best of" list articles under Things to do, Restaurants,
Shopping, Bars and pubs, Hotels and News, and monthly event pages. A place is an item of a list;
some venues have a page of their own. No place page was read for this record.

**Terms ([the access table](../site-access.md), 2026-10-02).** `www.timeout.com`: `refused`
(terms bar use by or for AI). `robots.txt`: open (`*` disallows user, ajax, rss, search and
booking paths; nine old bots are named, none of Anthropic's). Terms: bar use by or for AI, at
https://www.timeout.com/terms-of-use. Section 11, Intellectual property rights; "last updated in
October 2024". Training and retrieval-augmented generation are both named. The clause, as the
table has it: "Time Out does not permit the use of the Content on any artificial intelligence
(AI) or machine learning platform, tool, software or system in any way (including without
limitation any use of the Content for training or as part of retrieval-augmented generation),
without our prior written approval." The same section, read for this record before the table
existed. § 11.2: "You may view the Website on your computer screen and print its contents on
your printer for your personal and non-commercial use only. … You may not use the Website or the
Content in any other way, such as to transfer, copy, publish, reproduce, modify, or create new
works from, any part of the Content". And after the clause the table quotes: "You are also not
permitted to give archived or cached data sets containing the Content to another person or
entity."

**What decides it.** The access table: `refused`, on the site's own terms, which name training
and retrieval-augmented generation both. The surveys are run by a model, so nothing read there
may be used. Terms 0: the verdict is `veto` and the status `refused`. Identity is 0 as well. For
permission the terms ask for prior written approval; the site's contact page is
`/about/contact-us`.

**What the survey measured.** The Delhi survey read the Delhi front page and ten articles by
fetch; the Istanbul, Bali, Havana, Kyoto and Sydney surveys read pages of the same publisher's
editions. It was read before its terms were. Nothing of it counts: the surveys withdrew every
count taken from it, and this record carries no figure. The surveys withdrew, by region: Bali:
74 counts; Delhi: 105 counts; Havana: 21 counts; Istanbul: 89 counts; Kyoto: ten pages read
before its terms were, nothing counted; Sydney: 27 pages read before its terms were, nothing
counted.
