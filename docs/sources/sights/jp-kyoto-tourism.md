# Another Kyoto

The prefecture's guide to Kyoto outside the city, in English, Chinese and Korean. Looked at on
2026-10-02 because the Kyoto survey read it as the prefecture's source.

**What it sorts its places into.** Discover Another Kyoto; Sightseeing Spots; Inspiration;
Things to do (Events, Activities, Trip Ideas, Adventure Tours); Plan Your Trip (Accommodation,
Restaurants, Local Goods, Souvenirs). The survey found its listings to be directories and read
two pages. Only the home page was read for this record.

**Terms ([the access table](../site-access.md), 2026-10-02).** `www.kyototourism.org`: `allowed`
(copyright paragraph on the privacy page; silent on automated reading). `robots.txt`: open (`*`:
/wp-admin/ only). Terms: silent on automated reading, at
https://www.kyototourism.org/en/privacy/. The only legal page is the privacy policy, which has a
copyright paragraph: "Any unauthorized secondary usage, for profit or otherwise, of copyrighted
work appearing on this website, including reproduction, reprinting, loaning, distribution, and
sale of content is strictly prohibited." Nothing on automated access.

**What decides it.** Not decided: identity and coordinates were not looked at, so the verdict is
`provisional`. Its Sightseeing Spots listing is the part to open before the next survey of the
prefecture: a directory counts for nothing in a survey and is what the register looks for. The
copyright paragraph reserves any secondary use: ask first.

**What the survey measured.** Two pages by fetch: `discoverkyoto` and the titles of
`itineraries`. The Kyoto survey of 2026-10-02 counted it on 5 of the 246 entries of
`db/catalogue-coverage/expectations/kyoto.jsonl` (by type: place 2, activity 1, drink 1, food
1). On none of them is the surveyor's own list the only other source. The kinds in the front
matter are those under which the survey filed two or more of the entries it is named on.
