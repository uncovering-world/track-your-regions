# Armenia Travel

"The official website created by the Tourism Committee of the Ministry of Economy of the
Republic of Armenia" (its own terms), in English, Russian, German, French and Armenian. Looked
at on 2026-10-02 because the Yerevan survey read it in its second pass as its source from a
public body inside the country.

**What it sorts its places into.** "Places to Go": The Capital City of Yerevan, Top
Instagrammable Spots, Top Churches & Monasteries, Top Natural Sites, Top Cities, UNESCO Sites,
Top Fortresses, Top Archaeological Sites, Top 10 Attractions, Top Villages. Beside them "Things
to do" pages (museums and galleries, cuisine, wine), an events calendar and a blog. The Yerevan
page, the one read for the row, is prose under a few headings: a place is a name in a paragraph,
with no page, id or map point.

**Terms ([the access table](../site-access.md), 2026-10-02).** `armenia.travel`: `refused`
(terms bar robots, automated access or mining, with no leave for an AI agent). `robots.txt`:
some paths closed (`User-agent: *` only; English pages are not disallowed). Terms: bar automated
access, bar mining, at https://armenia.travel/privacy-policy/. The only legal page the footer
links is called "Privacy Policy"; under "Intellectual property rights and limited license" it
holds the terms. The bar is on systematic collection. No date. The clause, as the table has it:
"Your herein-mentioned license grant does NOT include the right for you to: (i) publish,
publicly perform and/or display, and/or distribute to any third party any Materials, including,
but not limited to, reproduction on any computer network and/or broadcast or publications media;
(ii) market, sell and/or make commercial use of the site and/or any Materials; (iii)
systematically collect and use of any data and/or content including the use of any data spiders,
robots, and/or similar data gathering, mining or extraction methods;" The same page, read for
this record before the table existed: "The use of the pages or the material contained therein
for other than non-commercial purposes is prohibited without prior written permission from us."
Footer: "© 2026 Armenia Travel. All Rights Reserved."

**What decides it.** The access table: `refused`, on the site's own terms. Clause (iii) of its
licence excludes systematic collection by "data spiders, robots, and/or similar data gathering,
mining or extraction methods", and clause (i) excludes showing its material to others, with no
leave for an agent reading on a person's behalf. Terms 0, so the verdict is `veto` and the
status `refused`. Identity and coordinates are 0 as well: a place is a name in a paragraph. What
would reopen it is the Tourism Committee's written permission; the site offers a contact form
(`/contact-us/`) and no address.

**What the survey measured.** The Yerevan survey read 21 pages of it by fetch in its second pass
and named it on 118 entries. It was read before its terms were. Nothing of it counts: the
surveys withdrew every count taken from it, and this record carries no figure. The surveys
withdrew, by region: Yerevan: 118 counts (the licence on its privacy-policy page).
