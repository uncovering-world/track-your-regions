# AA Traveller

The travel guide of the country's automobile association, written in New Zealand: road trips
"packed full of Must-Do's, Kiwi Gems and unexpected things" (its own description). Looked at on
2026-10-02 because the Auckland survey read it as its guide written by residents. It is filed as
`association`: a members' body, neither public nor a firm.

**What it sorts its places into.** Editorial pages (`/travel/editorial/…`): so many free things
to do, so many places to eat, so many "Kiwi Gems" of a region; and members' deals. A place is an
item of a page, under one of the association's labels.

**Terms ([the access table](../site-access.md), 2026-10-02).** `www.aa.co.nz`: `allowed`
(website terms silent on automated reading). `robots.txt`: open (`*`:
/content/experience-fragments/; SemrushBot and one other crawler disallowed). Terms: silent on
automated reading, at https://www.aa.co.nz/site-info/terms-and-conditions/website/. The clause
quoted is about copying, not about reading; nothing on robots, automated access or AI. Last
updated 14 July 2026. The clause, as the table has it: "You may view, download and print Content
for your personal, non‑commercial use only. You must not copy, reproduce, modify, distribute or
otherwise use any part of the Website for commercial purposes without AA’s prior written
consent."

**What decides it.** Identity 0, so the verdict is `curator-list`; the total is `unknown`
because the coordinates were not looked at. The terms keep the content to personal,
non-commercial use and ask written consent for commercial use: ask first.

**What the survey measured.** Forty-four pages by fetch, found in the site's sitemap. The
Auckland survey of 2026-10-02 counted it on 130 of the 235 entries of
`db/catalogue-coverage/expectations/auckland.jsonl` (by type: place 106, activity 11, route 8,
drink 2, event 2, work 1). On 5 of them nothing else but the surveyor's own list names the
entry. Places among those: Parnell Rose Gardens; Te Uru Waitākere Contemporary Gallery. The
kinds in the front matter are those under which the survey filed two or more of the entries it
is named on.
