# Kyoto Travel

The city's official guide, "© City of Kyoto and Kyoto City Tourism Association", managed by the
association. The edition read is the one for visitors from abroad, in English with Spanish,
French, Korean and Chinese; it carries no Japanese. Looked at on 2026-10-02 because the Kyoto
survey read it as the city's own source.

**What it sorts its places into.** About Kyoto (Discover Kyoto, Areas, Seasonal Information);
Things to Do (Travel Inspiration, Festivals & Events, Activities, History & Religion, Art &
Culture, Food & Drink, Morning & Night, Nature & Outdoors); Places to Visit (Destinations,
Hidden Gems of Kyoto, Sample Itineraries). The survey treated the destinations search, the
events calendar and the bookable activities as directories and counted a place where an area or
theme page shows it as that page's destination or names it in prose. Only the home page was read
for this record, so what a destination's page carries is not known here.

**Terms ([the access table](../site-access.md), 2026-10-02).** `kyoto.travel`: `allowed`
(copyright paragraph on the privacy page; silent on automated reading). `robots.txt`: open (`*`:
/wp-admin/ only). Signal: /llms.txt exists: "Generated by All in One SEO v4.9.8, this is an
llms.txt file, used by LLMs to index the site.". Terms: silent on automated reading, at
https://kyoto.travel/en/privacy-policy. The footer links a privacy policy and a translation
disclaimer; the privacy policy has a copyright paragraph: "Unauthorized use of any text or
images from the pages of this web site (Kyoto Travel) is prohibited." Nothing on automated
access.

**What decides it.** Not decided: identity and coordinates were not looked at, so the verdict is
`provisional`. The copyright paragraph reserves text and images: ask first.

**What the survey measured.** Twenty-eight pages by fetch: `discover`, the five theme pages,
`areas` and the thirteen area pages, `itineraries` and its six itineraries, `seasonal-info`. The
Kyoto survey of 2026-10-02 counted it on 128 of the 246 entries of
`db/catalogue-coverage/expectations/kyoto.jsonl` (by type: place 85, event 12, food 11, activity
10, route 4, object 4, drink 1, work 1). On 1 of them nothing else but the surveyor's own list
names the entry. The kinds in the front matter are those under which the survey filed two or
more of the entries it is named on.
