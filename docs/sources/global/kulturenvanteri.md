# Kültür Envanteri

A volunteer inventory of cultural heritage begun in 2019, with Ottoman, Byzantine, Hittite and
classical monuments at its core and entries across Europe. Looked at by #1977 because Wikidata
links it from half of the admitted Places of worship (544 of 1,086), 38 % of Archaeology and
14,768 castles and fortifications. Of the 70,175 places with a coordinate that carry the
identifier, a Wikivoyage edition uses 11.4 %, against 2.0 % of all places; 14.4 % of the
archaeological sites, 17.8 % of the places of worship and 38 % of the palaces among them.

**Terms (read 2026-10-25).** The terms page (Kullanım Koşulları) allows use of the content with
the source credited: "Kaynak belirtmek suretiyle içeriğin kullanılmasında herhangi bir sakınca
bulunmamaktadır." It forbids the route a sync would take. In the page's words, copying,
collecting or moving "tüm verilerin, metinlerin ve görsellerin bot, örümcek (spider), tarayıcı"
by "'içerik kazıma' (web scraping)" into databases without permission "kesinlikle yasaktır".
Photographs stay their uploaders' property. `robots.txt` closes only `/wp-admin/`.

**Verdict.** Refused as a source to read, on the scraping clause (terms 0). What remains usable
is Wikidata's own statement that an item carries a Kültür Envanteri ID, which is Wikidata's data
under CC0 and says only that the inventory has the place.
