# The Skyscraper Center

The tall-building association's database: completed, proposed and demolished buildings with
their measured heights, completion years and ranks. It is the reference by which "the world's
tallest" is decided. Looked at by #1977 as the source of the Skyscrapers kind's facts and of a
within-kind signal. Wikidata links it from 2,571 of the 4,526 places in the skyscraper pool and
from 43 % of the admitted Towers.

**Access and terms (2026-10-25).** `robots.txt` on www.skyscrapercenter.com closes only
`/map/`. The site links no terms page: the footer carries "© 2026 Council on Vertical Urbanism"
and a privacy policy on verticalurbanism.org. No dataset or API is published. A height read from
it would have to come from its pages, under terms that are not stated.

**Verdict.** Provisional. Before a Skyscrapers issue reads anything, the association's terms
have to be asked for. Until then, a building's height comes from Wikidata (P2048). The fact
that the association lists the building comes from Wikidata's P1305.
