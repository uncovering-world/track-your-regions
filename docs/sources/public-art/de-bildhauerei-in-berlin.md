# Bildhauerei in Berlin

A catalogue of sculpture in Berlin's public space, kept by HTW Berlin's museum studies
programme. Each object page gives the location (as an address, with a map link and a
coordinate), the artist, the date and the epoch. It also gives the state of completeness and the
condition. The site counted 3,267 objects on 2026-10-25. It was looked at by #1992 as the
regional tier for Public art in Berlin, one of the reference cities.

**Completeness.** It covers sculpture, monuments and fountains, which is the kind's older half,
across every district. An example is Mehmet Aksoy's group at Schlesische Straße and
Oberbaumstraße (1986–87). It does not hold street art. The Berlin list files three Public art entries
under the line. Two are outside its scope: the RAW-Gelände and Teufelsberg. Whether it holds the
third, the World Clock on Alexanderplatz, was not checked.

**Signal.** None. It is an inventory, and a world-famous work and a park bench figure are one row
each. It could fill identity and coordinates for a work another signal admits. It cannot say
what to admit.

**Terms (read 2026-10-25).** The legal notice names HTW Berlin as publisher and is silent on
robots, scraping and AI. No licence covers the texts or the data, and the footer reads
"© 2026 Bildhauerei in Berlin". The photographs on an object page are credited CC BY 4.0. Using
the data would need the publisher's permission, which scores terms 1 ("ask first"). No dump or
API exists, so a sync would scrape the WordPress pages.
